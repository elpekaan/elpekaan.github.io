stark-backup privacy policy

stark-backup uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. With it, the tool can see and change only the files it creates in its owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other file.

The tool uploads encrypted backup files and reads them back to restore them. It reads no other Google data.

No data is shared with anyone, sold or used for advertising. The backups are encrypted before they leave the server, so Google cannot read their contents either.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
