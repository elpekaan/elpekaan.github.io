workspace-mcp privacy policy

workspace-mcp uses the read-only Google scopes gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly. It reads its owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar data only on the owner's request or schedule, and never modifies it.

The data is kept only on the owner's own server. It is not sold or shared with third parties. It may be processed by the AI model services the owner uses, only to produce the owner's own summaries.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
